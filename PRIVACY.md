## 中文

**重生（Rebirth）隐私政策**

最后更新：2026-11-05

《重生》（Rebirth · 1 in 8 Billion）由独立开发者 Marvin 发行。本政策说明游戏会
处理哪些数据、为什么处理，以及你有哪些选择。

### 我们不收集什么

- 没有账号系统，不要求注册或登录。
- 不索取姓名、邮箱、电话号码、通讯录、照片、精确位置。
- 不读取或上传你的**人生档案**：出生记录、逐年事件、抉择、收藏时刻、设置，
  全部保存在设备本地存储（PlayerPrefs / 浏览器本地存储）。卸载应用即删除。
- 游戏本体离线可玩；只有加载广告和发送匿名统计时才需要联网。

### 我们会处理的匿名数据

| 接收方 | 用途 | 涉及的数据 |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob（仅 Android 版） | 展示插屏广告、反作弊与结算 | 广告标识符、设备型号与系统版本、IP 地址、粗略位置、广告展示/点击等互动数据 |
| GameAnalytics（Android 与网页版） | 了解玩法平衡与留存 | 匿名会话与事件：一生开始/结束、做出抉择、分享摘要；随事件附带的是**游戏内生成的虚拟数值**（终年年龄、抽到的国家 ISO3 代码、死因、抉择编号、选项序号、游玩时长、多人数），不是你的真实身份信息 |
| itch.io / Google Play | 分发与付款 | 由平台按各自政策处理，我们不接触你的支付信息 |

我们不会把上述数据用于出售，也不会把广告/统计数据与你的真实身份关联。

### 第三方 SDK

本应用包含 Google Mobile Ads（AdMob）与 GameAnalytics 两个第三方组件。
它们按各自隐私政策处理数据：

- Google（AdMob / 广告）：https://policies.google.com/privacy
- GameAnalytics：https://gameanalytics.com/privacy-policy

### 你的选择

- **广告**：Android 系统「设置 → 广告」可重置广告标识符、选择退出个性化广告。
  退出后广告数量不变，但相关性会下降。
- **统计**：不想发送匿名统计，可在设备/浏览器中阻断网络访问；游戏本体仍可离线游玩。
- **删除**：如需请求删除与该设备关联的匿名分析数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。
  本地存档无需申请，卸载应用即删除。
- **欧洲经济区 / 英国 / 加州用户**：你享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理与反对处理的
  权利，以及向监管机构投诉的权利。请通过下方邮箱提出请求。

### 儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，不会有意收集儿童信息。内容包含死亡、疾病与贫困的
叙述，建议 13 岁以上游玩。

### 变更

若未来版本引入账号、内购、在线房间或新的数据收集，本政策会在该版本发布前更新，
并在商店页面注明。

## English

**Rebirth — Privacy Policy**

Last updated: 2026-11-05

*Rebirth · 1 in 8 Billion* is published by independent developer Marvin. This
policy explains what the game processes, why, and what choices you have.

### What we do not collect

- There is no account system and no sign-in.
- We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise
  location.
- We do not read or upload your **life archive**: birth record, yearly events,
  decisions, saved moments and settings all stay in local storage on your
  device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- The story itself plays offline. A connection is only used to load ads and
  send anonymous analytics.

### Anonymous data we do process

| Recipient | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob (Android build only) | Serving interstitial ads, fraud prevention, billing | Advertising ID, device model and OS version, IP address, coarse location, ad impression/click interactions |
| GameAnalytics (Android and browser builds) | Understanding balance and retention | Anonymous session and design events: life started/ended, decision made, summary shared. Attached values are **in-game fictional numbers** — age at death, drawn country ISO3 code, cause of death, decision id, option index, session length, player count — never your real identity |
| itch.io / Google Play | Distribution and payment | Handled by the platform under its own policy; we never see your payment details |

We do not sell this data, and we do not link ad or analytics data to your real
identity.

### Third-party SDKs

The app contains Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and GameAnalytics. Each processes
data under its own privacy policy:

- Google (AdMob / Ads): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- GameAnalytics: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy-policy

### Your choices

- **Ads**: On Android, Settings → Ads lets you reset your advertising ID and opt
  out of personalised ads. You will still see the same number of ads, only less
  relevant ones.
- **Analytics**: Block network access for the app/browser to stop anonymous
  events; the game itself remains playable offline.
- **Deletion**: To request deletion of anonymous analytics tied to your device,
  contact us at the address below. Local saves need no request — uninstalling
  removes them.
- **EEA / UK / California residents**: you have the rights to access, correct,
  delete, restrict and object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory
  authority. Contact us at the address below.

### Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
their information. It contains narration about death, illness and poverty and
is recommended for ages 13+.

### Changes

If a future version adds accounts, in-app purchases, online rooms or new data
collection, this policy will be updated before that version ships and noted on
the store page.

Contact: wuyou517@gmail.com
